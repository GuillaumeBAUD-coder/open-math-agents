# The result

We resolve three interconnected open conjectures of Hugo Pfoertner concerning self-avoiding walks and disk-enclosed subsets of the planar triangular (hexagonal-center) lattice $\Lambda \cong \mathbb{Z}^2$ with squared Euclidean norm $Q(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2$, proving [OEIS A346126](https://oeis.org/A346126) and [OEIS A122226](https://oeis.org/A122226) and refuting [OEIS A346784](https://oeis.org/A346784) at $n = 10$. All three theorems are kernel-verified in Lean 4.33.1 across 7,933 lines and 390 top-level declarations in [PR #10631](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/10631), [PR #14284](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/14284), and [PR #14330](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/14330). The full archival preprint and Lean 4 verification bundle are published on Zenodo at [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24629756](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24629756) ([record 24629756](https://zenodo.org/records/24629756)).

- **Unifying Master Classification Theorem:** For every center $o \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and every real radius $r \ge 0$, every disk intersection $S_0 = \{p \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : Q(p - o) \le r^2\}$ with $|S_0| \ge 3$ is either Hamiltonian or a lattice translate of the 13-vertex Star of David $\mathcal{D}_{13}(v) = D(v, \sqrt{3}) \cap \mathbb{Z}^2$.
- **OEIS A346126 proved (`OMA.solution` in `proofs/oeis-a346126.lean`, PR `#10631`):** At every record length $m \ge 2$, every valid self-avoiding $m$-walk attaining the smallest enclosing disk is closed ($p(0) = p(m)$).
- **OEIS A122226 = A125852 proved (`OMA.solution` in `proofs/oeis-a122226.lean`, PR `#14284`):** For every integer diameter $n > 1$ and every center $o \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the disk subset $D(o, n/2) \cap \mathbb{Z}^2$ avoids $\mathcal{D}_{13}$ at every onion layer and is unconditionally Hamiltonian, proving $\operatorname{A122226}(n) = \operatorname{A125852}(n)$ for all $n > 1$.
- **OEIS A346784 refuted at $n = 10$ (`OMA.disproof` in `proofs/oeis-a346784.lean`, PR `#14330`):** At squared radius $s = 3$, $\mathcal{D}_{13}(0)$ achieves the 10th point-covering record ($k = 13$, with records $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13\}$), yet no valid $13$-walk fits in any disk of squared radius $s < 13/4$, so positive walk-length records skip $13$ and land on $m = 14$ at rank $9 = 10 - 1$ ($\{1, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14\}$), giving $13 \ne 14$.

# What was already known

- Reay and Zamfirescu (2000) proved that every $2$-connected linearly convex triangular grid graph except $\mathcal{D}_{13}$ is Hamiltonian, and Gordon, Orlovich, and Werner (2008) extended this classification to all connected, locally connected triangular grid graphs with the same unique exception $\mathcal{D}_{13}$, using planar-embedding topology.
- Hugo Pfoertner conjectured on OEIS that every optimal record walk of length $m \ge 2$ is closed (A346126), that integer-diameter maximum walk lengths equal maximum disk-covered site counts for all $n > 1$ (A122226 = A125852, September 2006), and in the comments of A346784 (numerators of minimal squared radii covering record numbers of hexagonal lattice points) that the $n$th record point count equals $\mathrm{A346126}(n - 1)$ for all $n > 2$.
- No prior proof connected disk-enclosed triangular lattice subsets to $\mathcal{D}_{13}$ across all real and integer diameters, nor established the exact first divergence of A346784 from A346126 at $n = 10$.

# Idea of the proof

## Zero-topology onion peeling and Master Classification

For $o \in \mathbb{R}^2$, $r \ge 0$, and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, define the $k$th discrete onion layer $S_k(o, r) = \{p \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : \forall d \in \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z},\; Q(p + k e_d - o) \le r^2\}$. Elementary convexity of $Q$ proves the exact iterated-interior identity $\operatorname{Int}(S_k) = S_{k+1}$ and horizontal convexity of every layer. Whenever $r \ge k + 1$, sector polynomial inequalities in $D_6$-normalized coordinates show that every vertex of $S_k$ has a contiguous cyclic arc of at least $2$ neighbors in $S_k$ (local connectivity), and strict radial descent into the unit disk $D_1(o)$ proves global connectedness.

Rather than invoking the Jordan Curve Theorem, we define a canonical boundary dart bijection $\phi : \partial S \to \partial S$ stepping along the unique counterclockwise start direction of each boundary vertex's neighbor arc. Analyzing the lexicographic minimum $u^*$ of any $\phi$-orbit via horizontal convexity and row barriers proves that every orbit contains the global lexicographic minimum $u_{\min}$ of $S$, making $\partial S$ a single Hamiltonian cycle $C_{\mathrm{out}}$. Universal layer-merge lemmas then splice $\operatorname{Int}(S)$ into $C_{\mathrm{out}}$: when $\operatorname{Int}(S) = \{v\}$ we either splice $v$ into a boundary triangle or force $S = \mathcal{D}_{13}(v)$; when $\operatorname{Int}(S)$ is an adjacent pair or Hamiltonian cycle, a Universal Rhombus-Bridge Lemma splices it into $C_{\mathrm{out}}$; and when $\operatorname{Int}(S) = \mathcal{D}_{13}(v)$, the forced boundary edge $u_1 = v + (2, 0) \to u_2 = v + (2, 1)$ (with $v + (3, 0) \notin S$) directly splices an explicit $13$-vertex Hamiltonian path $P_{13}(v)$ inside $\mathcal{D}_{13}(v)$!

For real radius $r$ with $q = \lfloor r \rfloor$, linear Cauchy-Schwarz slab bounds $|2B(p - o, e_d)| < 2$ force pairwise $Q(p_2 - p_1) \in \{0, 1, 3\}$ on the core $S_q$, classifying $S_q$ as empty, $K_1$, $K_2$, $K_3$, or a Hamiltonian $4$-rhombus. Downward induction from $k = q$ to $0$ proves the Master Classification Theorem: every $|S_0| \ge 3$ is Hamiltonian or $\mathcal{D}_{13}(v)$.

## Proofs of A346126 and A122226

For **A346126**, an open optimal $m$-walk ($m \ge 2$) spans $|S_0| \ge m + 1 \ge 3$ sites. If $S_0$ is Hamiltonian, taking a prefix of its cycle yields an $(m + 1)$-walk in $D(o, \sqrt{s})$, contradicting maximality. If $S_0 = \mathcal{D}_{13}(v)$, then $2 \le m \le 12$ and the Parallelogram Law forces $s \ge 3$, contradicting minimality because an explicit $12$-cycle $C_{12}$ centered at $(1/3, 1/3)$ fits inside squared radius $7/3 < 3$.

For **A122226**, every integer diameter $n \ge 2$ gives a half-integer radius $r = n/2$. At the core $q = \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$, $r \le q + 1/2$ forces $3Q(p - o) \le 1$, so $S_q$ is a clique of size at most $3$. At any layer $k$ with $|S_{k+1}| = 1$, we have $r < k + 2$ and hence $r \le k + 3/2$; by the Parallelogram Law on $v + (1,1) + k e_0$ and $v - (1,1) - k e_0$, any $\mathcal{D}_{13}(v)$ layer would require $r^2 \ge k^2 + 3k + 3 > (k + 3/2)^2$. Thus $\mathcal{D}_{13}$ never appears, so every disk $D(o, n/2) \cap \mathbb{Z}^2$ is unconditionally Hamiltonian.

## Exact rational refutation of A346784 at n = 10

Using $\mathbb{Z}^2 \rtimes D_6$, any disk center $o \in \mathbb{R}^2$ reduces to the fundamental triangle $T_0 = \operatorname{conv}\{(0,0), (1/2,0), (1/3,1/3)\}$, where $Q(o) \le 1/3$ and $s \le 13/4$ restrict all covered sites to the $31$-point universe $U_{31} = \{Q(p) \le 7\}$. Linear Cauchy-Schwarz exclusion certificates, midpoint $4$-triangle subdivisions, disjoint incompatible pairs, and five exact rational barycentric circumcenter triples across $20$ triangles establish eleven exact thresholds T1-T11. In particular, Threshold T10 ($s < 13/4$, two-level $7$-triangle subdivision) forces either $|S| \le 12$ or $S \subseteq \mathcal{D}_{13}(0)$, while Threshold T11 ($s \le 13/4$, $4$-triangle subdivision) forces $|S| \le 14$.

Because $\mathcal{D}_{13}(0)$ contains the $7$-vertex independent set $W = \{(0,0), (1,1), (-1,2), (-2,1), (-1,-1), (1,-2), (2,-1)\}$ whose complement has only $6$ vertices, $\mathcal{D}_{13}(0)$ admits no $13$-cycle, so no valid $13$-walk fits in any disk with $s < 13/4$. Together with thirteen explicit rational witness lemmas (`wit_pts_1` through `wit_att_14`, covering the eleven record sizes plus the open $1$-walk and $6$-step prefix, including $C_{14}$ at $o = (1/2, 0)$ with $s = 13/4$), this yields exact record ranks $10$ for $k = 13$ in `pointsFit` and $9 = 10 - 1$ for $m = 14$ in `attainable`, refuting the A346784 point-count conjecture via $13 \ne 14$.
